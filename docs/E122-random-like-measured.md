## 2. ⭐⭐⭐ 与要求的比较（✓ 关键 ✓）

$$\text{要求 ✓（E121）}：\int_N^{N+Y}\Delta^2dx=o(hN)=o(N^{3/2})\ ✓$$
$$\text{实测 ✓}：\int|\Delta|^2\approx N\cdot Y\cdot|S|^2=10^6\cdot10^3\cdot(5.5\times10^{-2})^2\approx\mathbf{3.0\times10^{6}}\ ✗$$
$$\text{目标 }hN=10^9\ ✓\ \Longrightarrow\ \textbf{比值}\approx3.0\times10^{-3}\ \Longrightarrow\ \textbf{余量}\approx\mathbf{330\ \text{倍}}\ ✓✓$$
$$\text{渐近 ✓}：|S|\approx\sqrt{\frac{\log T}{2\pi T}}\ \Longrightarrow\ \int|\Delta|^2\approx\frac{N\log T}{2\pi}\quad\text{而}\quad hN=\sqrt N\,N\ \Longrightarrow\ \textbf{比值}\sim\frac{\log T}{T}\to0\ ✓✓$$
