## §1 ⭐ 【原文】**Corollary 1.1（全文主定理的精确表述）**
```
"A direct corollary of the **Carathéodory–Fejér theorem** is the following:
 **Corollary 1.1.** Let T ∈ M_{n+1}(ℂ) be a **Hermitian, positive semidefinite Toeplitz matrix of rank n**,
 and let ξ ∈ ker T. Then **all the zeros of the polynomial P(z) := Σ_{j=0}^n ξ_j z^j lie on the unit circle**." ✓✓✓
```
$$\boxed{\text{Hermitian PSD Toeplitz（size }n+1\text{、rank }n\text{）+ ker 向量}\ \Longrightarrow\ P\ \text{的根全在单位圆}}$$
