The fundamental work in Riemann zeta function theory is [1] where Selberg
created a method by which he proved, in particular, Theorems A-D for Ta <
H < T, a > 1/2. We shall essentially use his method in this paper and, where
Selberg's arguments are repeated, we shall indicate this and in a number of cases
omit the corresponding calculations. In addition, we have considered it necessary
to use the formulation of Theorems 2 and 3 of [1] in which the original formulae
connecting S(t) and Si(t) with segments of the Dirichlet series and zeros of £(s) are
proved. We also note that Titchmarsh gave considerable attention to S(t) and Si (t)
in his monograph [4], where certain of Selberg's theorems and also conditional
theorems (assuming the Riemann hypothesis) are proved (see also [13]).
