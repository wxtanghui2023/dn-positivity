## 英文模板

```
Subject: Endorsement request for arXiv math.NT submission

Dear Prof. [Last name],

I am writing to request your endorsement for an arXiv submission in
mathematical number theory (math.NT).

I have posted a manuscript on Zenodo (DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.22042837)
discovering a telescoping identity for Riemann zeta zeros:

    g_n(t) = cos(nθ) - cos((n+1)θ),   θ(t) = π - 2arctan(2t)

This identity yields D_n = Σ_γ g_n(γ) > 0, where γ runs over the
positive imaginary parts of non-trivial zeros. We prove positivity
strictly for n ≤ 43 (sum of positive terms) and establish the
asymptotic bound

    D_n ≥ 0.1934 · log n - O(1) > 0   (large n)

via a phase-region split into a positive main term (Si(π)/(2π)·log n)
and an alternating-series negative remainder (Leibniz bound
log n/π²). Numerical verification covers n ≤ 10^4 using the first
10^5 zeros of Odlyzko.

The work relates to explicit formulas in the style of Murty–Rath
(2018, Mathematika) and to Li's criterion. The full paper, code,
and data are openly available at:

    https://github.com/wxtanghui2023/dn-positivity
    DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.22042837

Would you be willing to review the manuscript and, if you find it
sound, provide an endorsement for an arXiv math.NT submission?

Thank you for your time and consideration.

Best regards,
Hui Tang
Independent Researcher
[Email]
```
