## 第一段：投稿信息

Dear Editor,

We submit the attached manuscript, *A Positive Spectral Discrepancy
Criterion Equivalent to the Riemann Hypothesis under a Distributional
Weil Framework*, for consideration as a research article in analytic
number theory. The manuscript is a self-contained exposition of a new
equivalence criterion for the Riemann Hypothesis (RH) within a stated
distributional Weil framework.

## 第二段：贡献描述

We construct a positive spectral discrepancy criterion which is
equivalent to RH under a stated distributional Weil framework.
Specifically:

1. we construct a single explicitly given test object $H_0$;
2. we establish an explicit spectral difference identity
$$Q(H_0)-Q'_{\mathrm{RH}}=\sum_\rho\Delta_H(\gamma_\rho,\delta_\rho);$$
3. we prove the rigidity mechanism
$\Delta_H(\gamma_\rho,\delta_\rho)>0$ for $\delta_\rho\ne0$ and
$\Delta_H(\gamma_\rho,\delta_\rho)=0$ for $\delta_\rho=0$;
4. we develop the distributional pairing framework that makes all
pairings well-defined without requiring $H_0\in L^1$.

## 第三段：技术范围说明

The Weil explicit formula is invoked as an external framework; the
manuscript verifies the legality of the pairings for the specific test
object $H_0$. The explicit formula itself is not reproven. The analytic
framework follows the tempered distribution setting (Schwartz,
Hörmander) with the explicit-formula extension due to Weil and Barner.

## 第四段：独立性声明

The manuscript does not claim a proof of RH without the stated analytic
framework; instead, it establishes an equivalence criterion within that
framework. The equivalence theorem is conditional on the stated
distributional framework and on the validity of the explicitly verified
analytic identities. Independent verification of the key lemmas by the
community is expected before any stronger claim is made.

We believe the construction of a single spectral rigidity detector and
its explicit positivity structure will be of interest to specialists in
analytic number theory and the theory of the Riemann zeta function.
