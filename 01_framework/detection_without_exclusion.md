## Core contribution
Not a proof of RH. A structural audit answering: **why do a large class of RH-type structures stably produce critical-line signals (detection), yet fail to produce an exclusion mechanism?**

Main proposition (scope-restricted): within the audited routes (zero-only statistics, density/difference-spectrum analysis, positive-kernel constructions, preliminary zero-prime coupling), no exclusion mechanism independent of RH was obtained; the common gap is the missing upgrade from observable/statistical/compatibility information to uniform rigidity.

**This is not a completeness theorem.** See Open Problems (04_open_problems/).
