The LP bound requires nonnegativity of certain expressions involving Krawtchouk polynomials, which is equivalent to positive semidefiniteness of M=M′+M′′M=M^{\\prime}+M^{\\prime\\prime}. Suppose that every C⊆𝔼C\\subseteq\\mathbb{E} with covering radius rr satisfies (λ0,…,λn)​β(\\lambda\_{0},\\ldots,\\lambda\_{n})\\beta. Then
