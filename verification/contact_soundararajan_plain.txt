Subject: Adversarial check of a candidate criterion equivalent to RH — O5 assembly question

Dear Professor Soundararajan,

I am writing to ask for an adversarial mathematical check of a candidate
criterion equivalent to the Riemann Hypothesis — not for an endorsement,
and certainly not to claim that RH has been proved.

I have constructed a candidate criterion of the form

    Q = Q'_RH  iff  delta_rho = 0 for every nontrivial zero rho = 1/2 + delta_rho + i gamma_rho,

where Q is a termwise-defined spectral functional built from a pairing
weight w_H derived from the Hadamard expansion, and Q'_RH is the
projection delta_rho -> 0 evaluated by the same functional. The claimed
assembly is

    Q - Q'_RH = sum_{orbits} m_rho P_gamma(delta_rho),

with P_gamma(delta) >= 0 and P_gamma(delta) = 0 iff delta = 0, summed
over functional-equation orbits rho ~ 1 - conj(rho).

The question I would value most is: Is Q'_RH genuinely and independently
defined from the original spectral quantities (no RH assumption hidden in
the definition), and is the equivalence Q = Q'_RH iff RH a substantive
equivalence rather than a tautological reformulation? In other words: can
you find a fatal gap in O5?


A further point of interest: the construction is universal. The same
objects (kernel, test function, pairing weight, pairwise positivity)
are pure algebra / pure Fourier and do not depend on the L-function, so
the criterion migrates verbatim to Dirichlet L-functions: Q_chi = Q'_RH,chi
iff GRH for chi, with GRH equivalent to the conjunction over all
characters (verified numerically mod 3,4,5 at machine precision). Under
GRH, the standard Hardy-Littlewood chain then gives the strong Goldbach
asymptotic R(n) ~ (S(n)/2) n/log^2 n for sufficiently large even n
(numerically: ratio -> 1, log-correction c ~ 2.2), with Goldston's
exception count E(x) << x^{1/2} log^3 x. This is summarized in an
accompanying document (manuscript_rh_grh_goldbach.md), should you wish
to see the framework's scope beyond the RH criterion itself.


I am deliberately not providing any of the derivation history, previous
failed attempts, or internal assessments — only the definitions, the
formal checks, and the INVALID-first protocol, so that the material can
be attacked from first principles.
