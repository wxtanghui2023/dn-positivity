Subject: Adversarial check of a candidate RH/GRH criterion — the equivalence question (O5)

Dear Professor Soundararajan,

I am writing to request an adversarial mathematical check of one specific
aspect of a candidate criterion equivalent to the Riemann Hypothesis —
not an endorsement, and certainly not a claim that RH is proved.

The construction: from the Hadamard expansion one obtains a pairing
weight w_H(gamma, delta) with closed form, and a pairwise discrepancy
P_gamma(delta) >= 0, P_gamma(delta) = 0 iff delta = 0 (all-positive
coefficients for gamma > 1/sqrt(5)). Defining

    Q       = -sum_rho m_rho w_H(gamma_rho, delta_rho)
    Q'_RH   = -sum_rho m_rho w_H(gamma_rho, 0)      (projection delta -> 0)

the claimed assembly is

    Q - Q'_RH = sum_{orbits} m_rho P_gamma(delta_rho) >= 0,

so that Q = Q'_RH iff every nontrivial zero of zeta lies on the critical
line (RH). The same objects, being pure algebra / pure Fourier, migrate
verbatim to Dirichlet L-functions: Q_chi = Q'_RH,chi iff GRH for chi.

The specific question I would value most is about the **equivalence
itself** — the step labeled O5:

1. Is Q'_RH genuinely and independently defined from the original
   spectral quantities, with no RH assumption hidden in its definition?
2. Is the projection spectrum (delta -> 0 at fixed gamma, multiplicity)
   a legitimate comparison functional — not a tautology engineered to
   produce positivity?
3. Are the orbit/multiplicity bookkeeping and the "equality iff all
   delta = 0" rigidity substantive, or is there a circularity?

In other words: can you find a fatal gap in the equivalence — a hidden
RH assumption, a double-counting of orbits or multiplicities, or a
projection that smuggles in the critical line?

I deliberately provide no derivation history or internal assessments —
only the self-contained paper (Section 1 objects and theorems, Section 2
GRH migration, Section 3 the Goldbach consequence), with an
INVALID-first protocol: try to prove it wrong.
