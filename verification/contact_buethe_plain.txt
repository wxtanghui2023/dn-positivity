Subject: Adversarial check of a distributional pairing in a candidate RH criterion — the C3 step

Dear Dr. Buethe,

I am writing to ask for an adversarial mathematical check of one specific
technical step in a candidate criterion equivalent to the Riemann
Hypothesis — not an endorsement. Your work on the Weil–Barner explicit
formula and on rigorous explicit bounds is directly relevant, so I would
value your attack on the **distributional pairing machinery** (step C3).

The setting (full details in the attached paper, Section 1):

- Test function
      H_0(t) = -(1/(4 pi)) log(1+t^2) + 1/(2 pi (1+t^2)),
  with H_0''(t) = O(t^{-2}) in L^1. Note H_0 is NOT in L^1 (log growth),
  so the naive global pairing <S, H_0> = <log|xi|, H_0''> diverges and
  is not used; instead all pairings are defined **termwise** through L^1
  Fourier products of the per-zero kernels with H_0.

- Per-zero kernel K_delta(t-gamma) = (delta^2-(t-gamma)^2)/(delta^2+(t-gamma)^2)^2,
  with Fourier transform \hat K_delta(u) = 2 pi^2 |u| e^{-2 pi delta |u|},
  and \hat H_0(u) = e^{-2 pi |u|}[1/(4 pi |u|) + 1/2]; the product
  \hat K_delta \hat H_0 = (pi/2)e^{-2 pi a|u|} + pi^2 |u| e^{-2 pi a|u|} is L^1.

The questions I would value most:

1. Is the termwise distributional pairing framework valid as stated —
   an illegal Parseval use, a missing boundary term, or a convention
   slip (2 pi Fourier normalization, delta = 0 singular-kernel
   treatment)?
2. Is the interchange of per-zero pairing with spectral summation
   (absolute convergence via sum |delta_rho| |H_0''(gamma_rho)| < infinity)
   legitimate under the hypotheses of the Weil/Barner explicit formula
   as invoked?
3. Does H_0 satisfy the hypotheses of the explicit formula framework you
   would use — or is there a hypothesis (analytic strip, decay, test
   class) that H_0 fails, making the invocation of the explicit formula
   invalid at some step?

In short: can you find an illegal Parseval use, a missing boundary term,
a convention slip, or a hypothesis of the Weil–Barner framework that H_0
fails to satisfy?

I deliberately provide no derivation history or internal assessments —
only the self-contained paper, with an INVALID-first protocol: try to
prove it wrong.
