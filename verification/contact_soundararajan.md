# Contact Letter — Kannan Soundararajan（O5 / equivalence / hidden-RH attack）

**Subject:** Adversarial check of a candidate criterion equivalent to RH — O5 assembly question

Dear Professor Soundararajan,

I am writing to ask for an **adversarial mathematical check** of a
candidate criterion equivalent to the Riemann Hypothesis — not for an
endorsement, and certainly not to claim that RH has been proved.

I have constructed a candidate criterion of the form

$$Q = Q'_{\mathrm{RH}} \iff \delta_\rho = 0 \text{ for every nontrivial zero } \rho = \tfrac12 + \delta_\rho + i\gamma_\rho,$$

where $Q$ is a termwise-defined spectral functional built from a
pairing weight $w_H$ derived from the Hadamard expansion, and
$Q'_{\mathrm{RH}}$ is the projection $\delta_\rho \mapsto 0$ evaluated by
the same functional. The claimed assembly is

$$Q - Q'_{\mathrm{RH}} = \sum_{\rho/\sim} m_\rho P_{\gamma_\rho}(\delta_\rho),$$

with $P_\gamma(\delta) = \delta^2 M_2/(2U^2 D_+D_-) \ge 0$ and
$P_\gamma(\delta)=0 \iff \delta=0$, summed over functional-equation
orbits $\rho \sim 1-\bar\rho$.

**The question I would value most is:** Is $Q'_{\mathrm{RH}}$ genuinely
and independently defined from the original spectral quantities (no RH
assumption hidden in the definition), and is the equivalence
$Q = Q'_{\mathrm{RH}} \iff \text{RH}$ a substantive equivalence rather
than a tautological reformulation? In other words: can you find a fatal
gap in O5?

I am deliberately **not** providing any of the derivation history,
previous failed attempts, or internal assessments — only the definitions,
the formal checks, and the INVALID-first protocol, so that the material
can be attacked from first principles.

The self-contained material (definitions O1–O5, checks C1–C5, theorem,
and an INVALID-first protocol) is available at:
