READ-OFF (conclusions generated from the numbers above)
====================================================================================================
  * mechanism A is linear: the predicted first sign change at gamma=100 is 628, which is
    2 pi * gamma = 628 to within a few percent, so h(n) ~ n/(2 pi).
  * mechanism B is quadratic: an off-axis zero at height gamma needs n of order gamma^2,
    i.e. h(n) ~ sqrt(n).
  * therefore the square law belongs to the tail-budget mechanism, not to the criterion;
    the same Li criterion also admits a linear-range treatment, and that treatment is the one
    whose inputs are unconditional.
  * refined breakthrough criterion: a probe must be scored on TWO axes -- h(n) (reach) and
    whether its inputs are unconditional. Improving h(n) while losing unconditionality is not
    progress; the target is a window-type mechanism with h(n) growing slower than linearly.
