PART 3 -- usability, and the decisive comparison
   The kernel does produce the radial factor, so the question is settled by which functionals exist:
     (i)  functionals built from the individual primes p_m^pm : NOT USABLE, because those primes are
          unknown for all m (their existence is exactly what Oppermann conjectures).
     (ii) summatory functionals, i.e. what the explicit formula can read: on those, the second
          difference at shift h = sqrt(x) gives one zero rho the contribution
             -x^rho (rho-1) (h/x)^2 (1 + O(1/x)) ,   magnitude ~ x^{beta-1} |rho|^2 = x^{delta-1/2}|rho|^2
          with delta = beta - 1/2.  This is precisely the factor computed in the earlier assessment,
          and it carries no information beyond it.

   the factor is absent, but because its presence does not change the comparison that must be won.
