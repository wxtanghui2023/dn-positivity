READING
  The explicit form calibrated here is
      |Sigma_p(X)|  <=  (1/(2 pi log p)) log X + C(p) ,
  with the leading coefficient universal in X and depending on p only through log p.  The
  measurements give the empirical C(p) as the excess of the maximum over the leading term, and
  the p-dependence quoted in the project's notes can be checked by the last table of part (3).
  Part (4) documents the resonance: a perturbation of one part in ten million multiplies the
  maximum by orders of magnitude, which is the sharpness phenomenon recorded earlier.
