[S3b] FUNCTIONAL-EQUATION PAIRING  rho=beta+i gamma  <->  1-conj(rho)=(1-beta)+i gamma
  paper Eq.15/24: c_m(beta) = -p_L^{beta-1/2} e^{i gamma ln p_L}/rho_m.
  Exact relation (algebra, verified numerically below):
     |c(1-beta)/c(beta)| = p_L^{1-2 beta} * |rho| / |1-conj(rho)|     -> equals 1 only when beta = 1/2.
     the two partners sit at the SAME k = gamma/(2 pi), so the observable is the PAIR SUM
        S(beta) = (p_L^{rho-2pi i k}-1)/(rho(rho-2pi i k)) + (p_L^{1-conj rho-2pi i k}-1)/((1-conj rho)(1-conj rho-2pi i k))
     which is exactly invariant under beta -> 1-beta (the two terms merely exchange).
