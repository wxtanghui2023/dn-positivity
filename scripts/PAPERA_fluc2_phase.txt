SUMMARY -- routes 1 and 6
==============================================================================

     suppressed, P(s<0.1) = 0.0009 vs Poisson 0.0952.
  2. ROUTE 1 identifies the mechanism: the phase-increment correlation rho = <cos Dphi>
     encodes the gap law at frequency lambda(gamma); in the decorrelated regime the

     It is NOT a proof: (i) increments are not iid; (ii) rho must be bounded uniformly.
     The required inequality is weak (rho <= 1 - 9.3e-6 at n=T0^2) -- it is exactly a
     quantitative pair-correlation / gap-distribution statement.

     BOTH sides of the split are at the sqrt(count) scale; the prime-side stationary
     points fill [1,T0] for every n >= T0^2, so no far-from-resonance region exists.
     ROUTE 6 gives a correct diagnosis, no bound.
