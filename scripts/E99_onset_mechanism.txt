(2) onset index n0 where the partner's growth matches a main term of size M
    r^{-n} = exp(eps*n/gamma^2) >= M   =>   n0 = gamma^2*log(M)/eps
     eps      gamma       n0 for M=1e2       n0 for M=1e6
     0.5      1e+03         9.2103e+06         2.7631e+07

    0.01      1e+03         4.6052e+08         1.3816e+09
    0.01      1e+06         4.6052e+14         1.3816e+15

  -> worst case eps of order one: n0 ~ gamma^2 (up to a log) = the quadratic law
  -> a nearly-on-line zero (eps -> 0) has onset FAR LARGER: it is invisible

Boundary: the onset estimate is heuristic. It explains the quadratic law as the
worst case over admissible configurations, but it is NOT a proof that no criterion
consuming only 'no off-line zeros up to height T' can beat T squared.
