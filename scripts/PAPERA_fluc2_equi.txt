ROUTE 2 -- THE u = theta(gamma) DENSITY AND ITS SMOOTH PART

  gamma(u) = (1/2) cot(u/2);  rho(u) = main'(gamma(u)) * |d gamma/du|
  (asymptotically rho(u) = log(1/u)/(2 pi u^2) (1 + O(u^2 log u))).

  mass int rho du over u in [8.830e-07,3.141589] = 2001050.502037   (mass main(T0)-main(0+) = 2001050.501974)

  => in u-space the SMOOTH part is provably O(T0^2 log(T0/2pi)/n) (the period-block
     identity: int_0^{2pi} cos = 0 and int_0^{2pi} u cos u du = 0), but the FLUCTUATION
     is the same object as before:  sum cos - J = int_0^{T0} cos(n theta) dS.  The
     u-parametrisation is exact and gain-free.

  STRUCTURAL NOTE: theta(t) = 2 arctan(1/(2t)) has branch points at t = +-i/2;
  in u-space the phase n theta(t) ~ n/t becomes exp(i n gamma^{-1}), whose only
  singularity in gamma is at gamma = 0 (essential).  The admissible-class obstruction
  is invariant: +-i/2 <-> 0.

ROUTE 4 -- ERDOS-TURAN FOR x_gamma = n theta_gamma/(2 pi) mod 1
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  D_N <= C ( 1/M + sum_{k=1}^{M} (1/k) |(1/N) sum_gamma e^{2 pi i k x_gamma}| ),  C = 2 used.
  Koksma: |sum_gamma cos(n theta_gamma)| <= 4 N D_N   (Var(cos 2 pi .) = 4).

  NOTE: |S_1| is dominated by the SMOOTH part of e^{i n theta}: at n=1e6, |S_1|/N = 5.7e-1
  while Re S_1 = sum cos = 1.4e4 = 7.2e-3 N.  So the ET sums are NOT calibrated by the
  allowance at small n; ET fails there (bound 1.9e7 > allowance 2.0e6).

  => at n = T0^2 the ET bound (5.7e5) is just BELOW the allowance (6.5e5), but 94% of it
     is the 1/M floor; with the same M the bound fails at n = 1e6 by a factor 9.5.
     The requirement is |S_k| <= delta N with delta ~ allow/(4 C N log M) = 0.0058 for
     every k <= M, M >~ 25.  The k = 1 term IS the required sum (with 2 pi n in place of n).
     ET therefore presupposes ~30 constant-factor bounds each as hard as the original.
     Loss: strictly more than the original problem (30 frequencies instead of 1).

SUMMARY -- routes 2 and 4
==============================================================================
  2. u = theta(gamma) is an exact reparametrisation: the smooth part becomes J(n),
     provably O(T0^2 log T0/n) for n >> T0 by the period-block identity, but the
     fluctuation is unchanged (sum cos - J = int cos dS).  No gain; the analytic
     singularity merely moves +-i/2 -> 0.
  4. Erdos-Turan needs |sum_gamma e^{i k n theta_gamma}| <= delta N, delta ~ 0.006, for
     k <= M >~ 25.  The k = 1 term is the required sum itself; ET is circular and asks
     for ~30 times more.  Measured |S_k| would just fit at n=T0^2 (5.7e5 < 6.5e5) but
     fail by 9.5x at n=1e6.
