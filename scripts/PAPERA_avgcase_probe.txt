WHAT THIS IMPLIES FOR THE QUADRATIC RANGE
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  The quadratic range needs positivity while
        S_n  >  n * B_{T0},     with B_{T0} = (1/2) sum_{gamma>T0} gamma^{-2}.
  With S_n = N(1 + eps_n) and the margin N / (n B_{T0}) ~ 2 at n ~ T0^2, the
  fluctuation eps_n must satisfy eps_n << 1 at n ~ T0^2.
