(3) IS THE MINIMUM ALWAYS AT THE LEFT ENDPOINT n = N_m ?
----------------------------------------------------------------------------

   R^n/(n log n) is increasing for n >= e (exponential beats polynomial), so the
   window [N_m, 5m N_m] can only help. Checked above: yes, the minimum sits at N_m.
   => widening the window by m costs NOTHING; the cost of m sits entirely in C(m) and
      in the 5m factor of the detection lemma, exactly as the source says of its own 5.
