   => the prime count overtakes the zero count and keeps accelerating: for a FIXED nonzero
      profile the number of primes at which it can vanish is finite.  So a bridge of the form
      'off-line profile vanishes on the primes' would indeed force the profile to vanish, and the
      entire content of the bridge therefore sits in the arithmetic statement that it vanishes.

  Part 1 confirms the modulus identity and shows the pairing is fatal to the 'eventually invisible'
  picture: every off-line zero drags in a mode of modulus exceeding one.  Part 2 exhibits the
  resulting unbounded negative excursion of the Li tail.  Part 3 shows the vanishing route is not
