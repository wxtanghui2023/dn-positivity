Reading: the onset is logarithmic in the genus and carries NO height parameter.
In the number field the same analysis gives n0 ~ gamma^2/eps, because the carrier
is the damping map rho -> 1 - 1/rho, whose deficit is eps/gamma^2 rather than eps.
This is the p=0 versus p=2 contrast, and it is a comparison of CARRIERS, not of
probes: the function-field carrier is the Frobenius eigenvalue itself.
