   READING
   The diagonal-only baseline is X N / 2.  If the exact sums sit at a fraction of it and the smooth
   model reproduces that fraction, then the off-diagonal is a smooth-density effect and the input
   needed is the classical density, which is unconditional.  If the smooth model instead sits far
   from the exact value, the off-diagonal is governed by the fluctuation of the zeros about their
   smooth positions, i.e. by rigidity, a strictly harder input.
